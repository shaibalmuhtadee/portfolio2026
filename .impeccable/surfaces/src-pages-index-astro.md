# Homepage surface brief

- Scope and mode: `/`, one-page portfolio, Experience.
- Audience and job: recruiters and hiring managers must establish backend/full-stack fit and credible professional scope within one minute.
- Actions: View experience leads. A sanitized Resume and LinkedIn are eventual conversions; GitHub supports evaluation. The Resume action remains absent until a safe PDF exists.
- Proof: Zebra's 16 months, authentication for 200+ robots, and 99% Rust integration-suite coverage; qualified private-project decisions follow.
- Direction and memorable moment: Split Evidence. Identity and professional proof coexist in the opening desktop viewport; mobile turns them into ordinary document flow. Approved comps: `.impeccable/mocks/canon-split-evidence-approved.png` and `.impeccable/mocks/canon-split-evidence-mobile-dark.png`.
- Constraints: text-only projects; no private contact data, unavailable actions, hidden content, framework hydration, cards, shadows, decorative code, or generic navy/teal imitation.

## Comp grammar

- Component language: flat semantic regions, editorial rows, one filled primary control, outlined secondary controls, underlined text links.
- Corners and lines: near-square 0.3rem control corners; 1px structural rules; no elevation.
- Type: Figtree variable sans, heavy compact display, workhorse body, uppercase section headings that are headings rather than eyebrow labels.
- Responsive rule: 38/62 split at wide widths; one column below the content-driven breakpoint; sticky identity only when its full height remains reachable.
