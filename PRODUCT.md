## Users

The primary users are recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Shaibal Muhtadee for software engineering roles. They often arrive from an application or search result, scan quickly on desktop or mobile, and need to establish role fit, professional credibility, location and work eligibility, and a clear contact path.

Technical interviewers are a secondary audience. They need enough qualified detail about Shaibal's contribution, decisions, and outcomes to identify useful interview topics even though the underlying projects are private.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio helps Shaibal secure a full-time software engineering role in 2026. It should present him accurately as an early-career software engineer with 16 months of professional internship experience and substantial backend, full-stack, systems, infrastructure, and applied-AI project work.

Success means a recruiter can understand Shaibal's fit and strongest evidence within a minute, then open his LinkedIn profile or a sanitized public resume. A hiring manager should be able to find credible, specific work worth discussing in an interview.

## Positioning

Shaibal is a software engineer focused on backend and full-stack systems who remains open to software engineering roles broadly. The portfolio leads with production evidence from Zebra Technologies, including authentication services supporting API access for more than 200 autonomous robots, then shows technical judgment from private projects through concise, qualified decision reasoning rather than unsupported claims or unavailable demos.

## Operating Context

- The site is a public, single-page professional portfolio at `shaibalmuhtadee.com`.
- Visitors may arrive from job applications, LinkedIn, GitHub, recruiter outreach, or search.
- LinkedIn is the primary public contact path. GitHub is a supporting profile link.
- Shaibal is based in Toronto and is open to Canada-wide remote work and appropriate U.S. opportunities, with backend and full-stack roles preferred.
- Public work-authorization wording must state that Shaibal is a Canadian citizen prepared to seek TN status for qualifying USMCA professional positions, or employer-sponsored H-1B. It must not imply current U.S. work authorization or guaranteed eligibility.

- Experience and project entries must be easy to add, remove, reorder, or disable without changing layout code.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Present one focused home page rather than a multi-route portfolio in the first release.
- Put verified work evidence before biography and broad skill lists.
- Use text-only project evidence in the first release. Render no empty or disabled Source or Demo controls.
- Keep Inokta, Searchington, ChromaMap, and GanttWise within the publication limits and claim qualifications recorded in `CONTENT.md`.
- Keep Inokta's contribution collaborative. Do not mention its current YC application.
- Do not publish Shaibal's personal email address or phone number in page content, metadata, structured data, or downloadable files.
- Do not publish the supplied resume PDF. It contains private contact details and lacks the required accessible public export. Shaibal will manually upload the sanitized public resume after Phase 6; this launch input does not block the remaining implementation phases.
- Do not add a contact form, blog, chatbot, page loader, custom cursor, animated background, analytics, or project-detail routes in the first release.
- Keep core content available without client-side JavaScript. Without scripting, CSS must still follow the operating-system color preference; only the manual theme override may be unavailable.
- Optimize for Google's Core Web Vitals thresholds and the tighter lab budgets in `BUILD.md`.
- Deployment provider selection remains open until the site is complete and reviewed.

## Brand Commitments

- Public identity: Shaibal Muhtadee at `shaibalmuhtadee.com`.
- Category: Software Engineer - Backend and Full-Stack Systems.
- Lead message: "I build reliable software systems."
- Voice: direct, specific, evidence-led, and appropriately early-career. Do not present Shaibal as a senior engineer, AI specialist, current student, or sole creator of collaborative work.
- Shaibal values simplicity, clear hierarchy, a visible resume action once the final file is available, and restrained interaction. Brittany Chiang's portfolio and Starfolio are references for those qualities, not templates or identities to copy.
- Selected visual direction: a familiar developer portfolio played straight, with conventions used confidently rather than disguised by a decorative concept.
- Selected composition: Composition A, "Split Evidence." A left identity rail supports a scrolling evidence column on desktop; tablet and mobile collapse into one ordinary document flow.
- Ship equally complete light and dark themes. A first visit follows the operating-system preference, and a valid manual choice persists locally.
- Keep one visible, text-labeled theme control in the identity utilities on desktop and beside the name in the compact mobile header while Resume is unavailable. Both themes must preserve the same hierarchy and meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Craft benchmarks: Brittany Chiang's portfolio and Starfolio. Match their clarity, finish, responsiveness, and ease of scanning without copying their exact identity, palette, or layout.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with professional evidence. Make the Zebra internship and its verified scope easier to find than biography or technology lists.
2. Earn trust through precision. Qualify metrics, distinguish individual from team contributions, and state private status without apology.
3. Make evaluation fast. A recruiter should find role fit, location, availability, strongest proof, LinkedIn, and GitHub without effort, plus Resume after the final manual upload.
4. Keep the site maintainable. Content files and validated data should absorb future jobs, projects, and section changes without a redesign.
5. Protect speed, access, and privacy. Static content, restrained interaction, inclusive behavior, and minimal public personal data are product requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Support keyboard-only use, visible focus, reduced motion, forced colors, 200% zoom, and 400% reflow without horizontal scrolling at 320 CSS pixels.
- Keep essential resume content in semantic HTML even when a public PDF becomes available.
- Use clear language and do not rely on color, hover, motion, or imagery to carry essential meaning.
